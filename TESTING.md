# Sri Sai Jewels — Shopkeeper Testing Guide
## Phase 1.6 Interactive Demo Testing Checklist

Welcome to the **Sri Sai Jewels Billing Application Demo**. This version is prepared specifically for testing the user interface, billing calculations, mobile layout, and invoice printing workflow.

> ⚠️ **Note**: This is a **TEST VERSION**. All data shown is fictional sample data. No real shop or customer data is being saved to a database.

---

## 📋 Shopkeeper Testing Checklist

Please try performing the following 15 tasks on your desktop, laptop, tablet, or mobile phone:

- [ ] **1. Open Dashboard**: Check today's sales summary cards, quick action buttons, recent bills, and metal rates.
- [ ] **2. Try Mobile Menu**: On a phone or tablet, tap the top-left hamburger menu (`☰`) to open and close the navigation drawer.
- [ ] **3. Navigate to Customers**: Search for an existing customer by name (*e.g., Priya*) or phone number.
- [ ] **4. Add a Demo Customer**: Tap `+ Add Customer`, enter dummy details (*e.g., Ravi Kumar, 98765 00099*), and save.
- [ ] **5. Navigate to Jewellery**: Browse the inventory list. Filter by Gold/Silver metal or Ring/Chain categories.
- [ ] **6. Add a Demo Jewellery Item**: Tap `+ Add Jewellery` and create a dummy item with gross weight and wastage.
- [ ] **7. Start a New Bill**: Click `+ New Bill` from the sidebar or dashboard.
- [ ] **8. Choose Customer**: Select a walk-in customer or choose a registered customer from the list.
- [ ] **9. Add Jewellery to Bill**: Select an item from inventory or enter custom weight, gold rate, wastage %, and making charges.
- [ ] **10. Edit Gold Rate & GST**: Change the gold rate or GST % and verify that the total updates automatically.
- [ ] **11. Select Payment Method**: Select Cash, UPI, Card, or Bank Transfer.
- [ ] **12. Generate Invoice**: Click `Save Bill & Generate Invoice`.
- [ ] **13. Preview A4 Invoice**: Check the A4 invoice preview for store name, customer details, item breakdown, and grand total.
- [ ] **14. Preview 80mm Receipt**: Switch format to `80mm Thermal` to view the compact thermal receipt preview.
- [ ] **15. Test Printing & Share**: Tap `Print A4` or `Print 80mm` to launch the browser print window, or tap `Share` to copy bill text.

## 💬 Shopkeeper Feedback Questionnaire

After completing the checklist, please share your thoughts on the following questions:

1. **Ease of Use**: Was creating a new bill clear and intuitive?
2. **Mobile Usability**: Did the screen fit comfortably on your phone/tablet without awkward scrolling?
3. **Invoice Layout**: Is the A4 invoice clear and easy for customers to read?
4. **Calculations**: Are the gold rate, wastage, making charge, and GST calculations presented correctly?
5. **What was confusing?**: Were any buttons or labels difficult to find or understand?
6. **Suggestions**: What feature or button would make counter billing faster for your shop?
